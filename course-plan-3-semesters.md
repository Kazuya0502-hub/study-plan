## 0. Five conclusions up front

1. **UC Davis EE is reachable from LMC.** In the actual ASSIST data both `EEC 007` (4 units) and `PHY 009D` (4 units) show **"No Course Articulated"**, but **Davis's own rule explicitly waives them in that case**: when the site shows "no course articulated", the requirement is waived for UC Davis admission and TAG and must be completed after enrolling. So **TAG survives**. Details and procedure: **`tag-davis-and-dream-schools.md`** (v4).
2. **CHEM 025 alone is not enough.** The agreement maps `CHE 002A` to the **CHEM 025 + CHEM 026 series (10 units)** and states that **the whole series must be completed at the same institution before transfer**.
3. **The physics mapping is the reverse of what we assumed.** `PHYS 042 -> PHY 9B`, `PHYS 041 -> PHY 9C`.
4. **With the waiver, the Davis plan fits in 13/15/16/18 units** (zero 19-unit terms). The earlier "two 19-unit terms" came from sourcing `EEC 7` and `PHY 9D` externally without knowing about the waiver. **But the waiver wording appears only in the Fall 2025 brochure, so written confirmation is mandatory.**
5. **As insurance, the UCR / UCSC / Merced requirements are also met.** Those three **do not require modern physics**, and LMC's COMSC 122/132/142 cover their CS requirements (UCSC requires COMSC 122 to have been taken in Fall 2026). Target: **first choice Davis TAG, insurance UCR.**

### 1.3 What follows

- Of the 13 Davis EE admission requirements, **2 cannot be met at LMC** (EEC 7, PHY 9D).
- **Both are waiver targets.** Davis's rule: "**When the ASSIST website indicates 'no comparable course' or 'no course articulated,' the selective major course requirement will be waived for UC Davis admission and TAG but must be completed after enrolling at UC Davis.**"
- So **Davis EE works for Fall 2028 transfer** (EEC 007, EEC 010, EEC 018, ECS 036B get taken at Davis).
- **Conditions**: (1) save the ASSIST screenshot every year, (2) state it in the TAG application comments, (3) get the advisor's written confirmation. Details in `tag-davis-and-dream-schools.md` section 1.

**If the waiver is denied (try in this order)**

1. **Bakersfield ENGR B19C** (EEC 007 equivalent). Fall 2026 has a **fully online, asynchronous** section. F-1 students cannot use the CVC Exchange, so this needs a direct application, non-resident tuition, and prior DSO approval at LMC.
2. **De Anza PHYS 4D** (PHY 009D equivalent). **One hybrid section in Spring 2027**, four mornings a week, about 60 miles.
3. **Transfer to Cabrillo** (CS 11M, PHYS 4D). The only college where both articulations are confirmed.
4. Delay to Fall 2029 (loses the two-year limit at dream schools, so it conflicts with the private-school applications).
