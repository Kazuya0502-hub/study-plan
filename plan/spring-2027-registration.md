### Why these choices

- **MATH 220 is 7411 (in person), not 7407 (fully online)**. An F-1 student may take online courses for "1 course or 3 units", and that allowance goes to COMSC 122. **Everything else is in person**, which also clears the 9-in-person-unit condition with room to spare.
- **CHEM 025 is 7480**. Section 7472 has a lab starting MW 7:55, which conflicts badly with everything else. 7480 is in the afternoon and does not clash with MATH 220 or COMSC 122 on Tuesday/Thursday. **If switching to CHEM 006, confirm the days for 9524 (Brentwood)**.
- **ENGL C1000 is 0312 (hybrid)**. Fully online sections (0315 / 0320 / 0343) are plentiful, but the online allowance is spent on COMSC 122. 0312 meets once a week in person and is the lightest choice.
- **COMSC 122 has no in-person section in Spring 2027** (only two online: 6940 async, 6944 live). 6944 forces two live sessions a week, which helps discipline. **6940 (async) is also acceptable** and still keeps the online allowance at one course.
- **The fifth course** is not needed for the 7-course pattern (already satisfied). Its purpose is to **clear the 30-units-before-summer wall** and to secure the two humanities/social-science courses Penn wants. Take a **UC-B or UC-H in person**.

**Structural constraint (no way around it):**

| Course | Prerequisite | Co-requisite |
|---|---|---|
| PHYS 040 | **MATH 220 (completed)** | none |
| PHYS 041 | **PHYS 040 (completed)** + **MATH 230 (co-requisite, may run concurrently)** | MATH 230 only |
| PHYS 042 | **PHYS 040 (completed)** + **MATH 230 (co-requisite, may run concurrently)** | MATH 230 only |

MATH 220 is Spring 2027, so **PHYS 040 can be no earlier than Fall 2027**. That forces both 041 and 042 into Spring 2028 (neither fits in Fall 2027).

**Both courses often run a single section per term, and in some years taking them together means clashing days.**

| Term | PHYS 041 | PHYS 042 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring 2027 | Thu 12:45-17:00 at Brentwood | Thu 14:40-17:50 at Pittsburg | **Clash** |
| Spring 2026 | Thu 12:45-17:00 at Brentwood | **Tue** 14:40-17:50 at Pittsburg | **Avoided** |

**Assume roughly a 50% chance of a clash in Spring 2028.** When the Spring 2028 schedule appears in November 2027, **check the days for 041 and 042 first**.
